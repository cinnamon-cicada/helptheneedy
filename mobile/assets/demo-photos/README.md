# Demo photography

These are licensed photographic illustrations of fictional demo identities and requests. The pictured people are not members, recipients, or endorsers. The app displays a persistent demo disclosure on every route and repeats it in gift confirmation sheets. No real payments are processed. Casual café, outdoor, and selfie-moment portraits make up most avatars, with a few pets and flowers for variety. User-uploaded photos are preserved.

The photographs were downloaded from Pexels on 2026-11-06 and 2026-11-07 under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/). The license permits app use and modification; it does not imply endorsement or grant unrestricted personality rights. `sources.json` records the original photo page (including photographer credit), download URL, license, retrieval date, and SHA-256 for every file. Request and earlier avatar images were downloaded at 1000px wide; new portraits use Pexels-served 600px square crops recorded in the manifest; no generative edits, filters, synthetic degradation, or compositing were applied. The cat avatar is cropped around the subject for small circles; its crop is recorded in the manifest. The app retains its existing cover crops.

Request photos depict an iced matcha by a laptop, shared delivery pizza, headphones in everyday use, takeaway noodles, a used sketchbook, a flower market, a flat white by a laptop, and an open chemistry textbook. Images illustrate the existing requests; they are not evidence of ownership, a price, or a real request for money.

`src/data/userAvatars.ts` assigns avatar photos once per user ID; Supabase seed IDs alias the same identities. `DemoProvider.avatarForUser` resolves the current account dynamically so profile edits update its leaderboard entries and authored comments immediately. Comments keep an author ID instead of a photo snapshot. Existing uploaded profile photos are preserved. `src/data/demoPhotos.ts` supplies bundled request copies for mocks and exactly the eight fixed seeded demo post IDs. This also replaces old seeded photos without requiring a database reset. Other Supabase posts and uploaded photos are unaffected. The seed uses the corresponding licensed remote URLs; rerunning it refreshes photo paths without changing gift history or progress.
